labor-charge-verifier

Check that the labor charges on a service-contract invoice are backed by timesheets, payroll and contract or wage-determination rates, before the invoice goes out.

Your files stay on your computer. The checks run inside this browser tab. Nothing you select is uploaded or sent anywhere. (The first time, the page downloads the open-source Python runtime, Pyodide, from a public content network.)

Loading the checking engine. The first time takes about 20 seconds.
  1. Choose a check below.
  2. Add your files (CSV, saved from Excel or your payroll system), or click Use sample data to try it with fictitious data first.
  3. Click Run, read the findings and download the report.

Invoice vs. records

Checks each invoice line against the employee list, timesheets, payroll and contract prices. Finds charges the records do not support and work that was left off the invoice.

Pay vs. wage determination

Checks each payroll week against the wage determination: hourly rate, health and welfare benefit, and overtime over 40 hours. Shows any amount owed to a worker.

FAR 52.222-43 price adjustment

Builds the worksheet for a price adjustment after a new wage determination, a draft notice to the contracting officer and a list of records to attach. Overhead, G&A and profit are not allowed by the clause and are refused. Read the plain-language guide.

Settings (optional)

The defaults come from config/settings.example.yaml. Review them before using your own data.

Use the health and welfare rate printed on the wage determination in your contract.

Add all files (or use sample data) to enable Run.

Results

Every finding is a lead for a person to review, not a conclusion. This tool does not replace the contract, the official wage determination, the contracting officer or an attorney. General information, not legal or accounting advice.
